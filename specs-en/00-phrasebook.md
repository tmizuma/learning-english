# Phrasebook — 場面別フレーズ帳

学んだフレーズをここに蓄積していく。

---

## Design Review — 提案と意見表明

### "I'd suggest we go with X because..."

**意味:** Xでいくのがいいと思います、なぜなら…
**使用例:** "I'd suggest we go with Redis because it gives us sub-millisecond latency."
**注意:** suggest の後は動名詞(-ing)。"suggest to do" は ❌

### "What I have in mind is..."

**意味:** 私が考えているのは…
**使用例:** "What I have in mind is using Redis as a caching layer."
**注意:** is の後は動名詞(-ing)が自然

### "The trade-off here is..."

**意味:** ここでのトレードオフは…
**使用例:** "The trade-off here is that we get faster reads but we take on extra infrastructure cost."
**注意:** "take on" で「（負担を）引き受ける」

### "The main benefit of this approach is..."

**意味:** このアプローチの主なメリットは…
**使用例:** "The main benefit of this approach is that we can keep product images nearly up to date while significantly reducing the load on our database."
**注意:** "while + -ing" で「〜しつつ、同時に〜」が便利
